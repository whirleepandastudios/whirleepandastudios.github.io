Snug Paws Rescue: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Snug Paws Rescue ("the game") is made by Whirlee Panda Studios ("we", "us"), based in Australia. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and, for players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

The shelter, the animals and the families in the game are fictional. The game is not a charity and does not collect donations: purchases in the game buy game items only.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or other personal details. To back up your progress, the game creates a cloud account for you automatically (an anonymous ID, no sign-up).
  • Your progress is saved on your device. It is also backed up to the cloud (Google Firebase, in the EU). On Android, when you're signed in to Google Play Games, it can come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. You can delete the cloud copy and the cloud account in Settings → Delete cloud save.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads. Our ad partners collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads may be personalised, and can change that in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, how many animals you've rehomed), so we can balance and improve it. Your privacy choice covers this too, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details. When cloud backup is on, our own server checks each purchase with Google before the game delivers it.
  • If you share a picture from the game, your phone's own share menu sends it where you choose. The game sends it nowhere by itself.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information the game collects

Saved on your device only

Your game progress (your animals, shelter, currencies and collections), settings and privacy choice are stored in a file on your device. It's removed when you uninstall the game. Your device's own backup service may include it in your personal backups.

Cloud save (Google Firebase)

The game backs up your progress to the cloud with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. The game creates a cloud account for this by itself; you don't sign up. On Android, if you're signed in to Google Play Games, the backup is tied to your Play Games account, so your progress can come back after you reinstall the game or move to a new Android phone. Without Play Games the backup protects against data loss, but a reinstall usually starts a new cloud account.

What's stored:

  • a copy of your whole save file, the same as on your device: your animals and shelter, currencies and items, collections, settings, your privacy and consent choices, your purchase records (the products you bought and the store's transaction IDs, never payment details) and the save's own random player ID;
  • when it was saved, the game version, and a random ID for this installation of the game (not a hardware or advertising ID);
  • a Firebase user ID: a random account ID the game creates without asking for any personal details;
  • on Android, if you're signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, which links the backup to your Play Games account;
  • like any Firebase sign-in, Firebase Authentication also receives your device's IP address, used by Google to keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse, and basic technical details (device model, OS and SDK versions).

It's stored by Google Firebase in the European Union (the EU multi-region). The game keeps the latest copy and up to 5 earlier ones. Each backup is kept until you delete it in the game, and a copy nobody has backed up for 24 months is deleted automatically: the automatic expiry removes the save copies and the data in them, while an empty account record (the random ID, with no game data) may remain until it is removed on request with your Cloud ID. Uninstalling the game doesn't delete the cloud copy: use Settings → Delete cloud save first, or see "Delete your cloud data" below. We use this only to keep your progress safe.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When analytics is allowed, the game sends gameplay events. For example: a session started, an animal was rescued, a care step was finished, an animal found a home, a room was restored, a rewarded ad was offered or watched, the Share button was tapped, or a purchase was made (the product and price, never payment details). Firebase also collects standard technical information, such as:

  • an app-instance identifier;
  • device model and operating system version;
  • app version;
  • country or region, derived from the IP address;
  • engagement time.

We use this only to understand and improve the game. The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive game settings (such as how long a care timer runs) so we can tune the game without an update; it uses the same app-instance identifier and doesn't collect anything else. Google describes its processing at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, or hits a serious error such as a damaged save file, Crashlytics sends a report with technical information: the error and where it happened, the device model, operating system version, app version and an installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks)

Rewarded ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads, the ironSource exchange (also part of Unity) and Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to personalise them if you allow it, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID);
  • your IP address;
  • device and network information;
  • how you interact with ads.

Their privacy policies apply: Unity (https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) and Google (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. If the tool can't load when you first open the game (for example, with no internet), the game asks as soon as it can, and until then shows no personalised ads and sends no analytics. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised unless you turn this off in Settings → Privacy choices. The game stores your choice on your device and passes it to the ad partners.

Ads are never shown unless you choose to watch one.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. The game receives a purchase receipt so it can deliver your item and restore purchases. We never see your name, payment card or billing details.

When cloud backup is on and the game is signed in to its cloud account, the game sends the purchase's product ID and Google Play's purchase token (a code that identifies that one purchase) to our own server (Google Firebase Cloud Functions, in the European Union), which asks Google Play whether the purchase is real and not already used, so forged or reused purchases aren't delivered. The request is signed in as the game's cloud account (see Cloud save). When cloud backup is off, or the game isn't signed in yet, the purchase is checked on your device only and nothing is sent to our server. Our server doesn't store the product ID or the token. Like any web request, Google keeps a standard technical log of the call for 30 days: the time, your device's IP address, the user agent (the app and your device's software version), how long the server took to answer and whether the call worked, but not the token.

Sharing a picture

When an animal is ready for a home, the game can show a before-and-after picture of it with a Share button. If you tap it, the game hands that picture to your phone's own share menu, and you choose where it goes (or cancel). The picture is made by the game and contains only game art and the animal's name. The game doesn't read your photos, contacts or files, needs no permission for this, and isn't told which app you picked or whether you sent it. If analytics is allowed, the game notes that the Share button was tapped.

Ratings

The game may once show Google Play's own rating window. It is run entirely by Google Play, and the game is not told whether or how you rated it.

Our other games

Settings may show a link to our other games on Google Play. It is a plain store link: tapping it opens Google Play, and the game shares no data with the other game.

Writing to us

Settings → Support and feedback opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. If you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply and to fix or improve the game. We delete the message when we no longer need it, or sooner if you ask.

Reminders

If you allow reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device, for example when an animal's paw has healed or a family is visiting. These are created on your device, and no data is sent anywhere for them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Why we use it (legal bases for EEA, UK and Swiss players)

  • Personalised ads and analytics: your consent. You give or refuse it on first launch and can change it at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, crash reports, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping it working.
  • Game settings (Firebase Remote Config): our legitimate interest in tuning the game without an update. It fetches game settings and uses no personal data beyond the app-instance identifier.
  • Purchases: to perform our contract with you (delivering what you bought), and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud (checking a purchase with Google before delivering it).
  • Cloud save: our legitimate interest in keeping your progress safe and bringing it back. You can object at any time by deleting it in the game (Settings → Delete cloud save), which also stops the backup on that device.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads and analytics: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent on first launch through Google's consent tool, where you can allow ads personalisation and analytics separately. Elsewhere, personalised ads and analytics are on unless you turn them off. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. If you decline, ads are still available but not personalised, and analytics collection is switched off. Until your choice is known, the game sends no analytics: events from that first session stay on your device and are sent only if analytics is allowed, otherwise they're deleted.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Reminders: you can refuse or switch off notifications at any time.
  • Cloud save: Settings → Delete cloud save deletes the cloud copy of your progress and your cloud account at any time; "Back up my progress" in the same place switches the backup on again.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes your saved progress on the device, but not the cloud copy (see "Delete your cloud data" below). Analytics data isn't linked to your name or email. Google deletes detailed event data automatically after 2 months, and user-level data after 14 months without play (the retention settings for the game's Analytics property). You can also email us (address below), and we'll help where we can identify your data.

Delete your cloud data

  • In the game: open Settings → Delete cloud save. This deletes your progress from the cloud (all copies) and your cloud account at once; your game on the device stays. If the account can't be removed straight away (for example you're offline), the game finishes it the next time you're online.
  • By email, for example if you no longer have the game: write to whirleepandastudios@gmail.com with your Cloud ID (shown in Settings → Cloud save; tap it to copy) or the Google Play Games account you used. We delete the cloud copy and the cloud account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. Players in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and Gibraltar can also ask us to delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it, and can withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what happened before). We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (www.oaic.gov.au) or, in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or Gibraltar, to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Our partners (Google, Unity and the app stores) may store and process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate, under their own safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). Cloud saves are stored by Google Firebase in the European Union, and purchases are checked on Google Firebase servers in the European Union (Google's technical logs of those checks are kept under Google's own terms and may be stored elsewhere).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when the game's data practices change and change the date above.
