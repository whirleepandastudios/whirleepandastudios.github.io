Crumbs & Clues: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Crumbs & Clues ("the game") is made by Whirlee Panda Studios ("we", "us"), based in Australia. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and, for players in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads. Our ad partners collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads may be personalised, and can change that in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, which chapter you've reached), so we can balance and improve it. The same privacy choice turns this off.
  • We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information the game collects

Saved on your device only

Your game progress (board, currencies, story progress), settings and privacy choice are stored in a file on your device. We don't receive this file. It's removed when you uninstall the game. Your device's own backup service may include it in your personal backups.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When analytics is allowed, the game sends gameplay events. For example: a session started, an order was served, a chapter or task was completed, a rewarded ad was offered or watched, or a purchase was made (the product and price, never payment details). Firebase also collects standard technical information, such as:

  • an app-instance identifier;
  • device model and operating system version;
  • app version;
  • country or region, derived from the IP address;
  • engagement time.

We use this only to understand and improve the game. Google describes its processing at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report with technical information: the error and where it happened, the device model, operating system version, app version and an installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks)

Rewarded ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads and the ironSource exchange (also part of Unity). To show and measure ads, and to personalise them if you allow it, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking on iOS);
  • your IP address;
  • device and network information;
  • how you interact with ads.

Unity's privacy policy applies: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Ads are never shown unless you choose to watch one.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The game receives a purchase receipt so it can deliver your item and restore purchases. We never see your name, payment card or billing details.

Reminders

If you allow reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device, for example when your energy is full. These are created on your device, and no data is sent anywhere for them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Why we use it (legal bases for EEA/UK players)

  • Personalised ads and analytics: your consent. You give or refuse it on first launch and can change it at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, crash reports, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping it working.
  • Purchases: to perform our contract with you (delivering what you bought).

Your choices

  • Personalised ads and analytics: on first launch the game asks whether you allow personalised ads and analytics. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. If you decline, ads are still available but not personalised, and analytics collection is switched off.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this later in iOS Settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes your saved progress. Analytics data isn't linked to your name or email. Google deletes detailed event data automatically after 2 months, and user-level data after 14 months without play (the retention settings for the game's Analytics property). You can also email us (address below), and we'll help where we can identify your data.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. Players in the EEA and UK can also ask us to delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it, and can withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what happened before). We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (www.oaic.gov.au) or, in the EEA or UK, to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Our partners (Google, Unity and the app stores) may store and process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate, under their own safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when the game's data practices change (for example, when cloud save arrives) and change the date above.
