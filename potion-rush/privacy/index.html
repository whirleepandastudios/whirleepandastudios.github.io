Potion Rush: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Potion Rush ("the game") is made by Whirlee Panda Studios ("we", "us"), based in Australia. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and, for players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.

The short version

  • You don't create an account and we don't ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads. Our ad partners collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, to show and measure ads. You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and change that choice in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, how long a shop takes), so we can balance and improve it. You can turn this off with your privacy choice (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a separate choice in Google's consent tool).
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information the game collects

Saved on your device only

Your game progress, settings and your privacy choice are stored in a file on your device. We don't receive this file. It's removed when you uninstall the game. (Your device's own backup service may include it in your personal backups.)

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When analytics is allowed, the game sends gameplay events, for example: a session started, a shop was started or completed (with the time it took), a station was unlocked, a goal was completed, a rewarded ad was offered or watched. Firebase also collects standard technical information such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, country/region derived from the IP address, and engagement time. We use this only to understand and improve the game. Google's processing is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks)

Rewarded ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads, the ironSource exchange (also part of Unity) and Google AdMob. To show, measure and (if you allow it) personalise ads, these partners may collect your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, the latter only if you allow tracking on iOS), IP address, device and network information, and how you interact with ads. Each partner's own privacy policy applies:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown or analytics is collected. Under Manage options you can choose purposes one by one: personalised ads need consent to storing and accessing information on your device, creating a personalised ads profile and using it to select ads; the game's own play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) need consent to storing and accessing information on your device, understanding audiences through statistics, and developing and improving services. (If Google's tool can't be reached the first time you play, for example when you're offline, the game asks its own question until the tool can be shown.) The tool stores your choice on your device and passes it to the ad partners. If you choose Do not consent, ads are still available but limited and not personalised, and no play statistics are sent.

Ads are never shown without you choosing to watch one.

Why we use it (legal bases for EEA, UK and Swiss players)

  • Personalised ads and analytics: your consent, which you give or refuse on first launch and can change at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping it working.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads and analytics: on first launch the game asks whether you allow personalised ads and analytics (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, through Google's consent tool, where ads and statistics are separate choices). You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy settings, which in those regions opens Google's consent tool again. If you decline, ads are still available but not personalised, and analytics collection is switched off.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this later in iOS Settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes your saved progress. Analytics data is not linked to your name or email, and Google deletes the detailed event data automatically after the retention period set in our Firebase project (2 months). You can also email us (address below) and we'll help where we can identify your data.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland can also ask us to delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it, and can withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what happened before). We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (www.oaic.gov.au) or, in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Our partners (Google and Unity) may store and process data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate, under their own safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when the game's data practices change (for example, when cloud save arrives) and change the date above.
